Bear formation calculator

Splits your troops across up to 6 bear hunt marches at a fixed composition ratio.

!
Before you set these in game
You can't set an exact troop number in the bear formation screen. It won't account for the bonus rally capacity. The best approach is to set the ratio and calculate using the resulting number. When you set the formation at the button you can double check the ratios. Full rally and lack of archer can modify the ratios.

How it's calculated
  1. Troop ceiling — the scarcest type relative to its share: T_troops = min(Inf / r_inf, Cav / r_cav, Arc / r_arc)
  2. Each march has its own cap: a march with a hero leader uses base deploy cap + 3,000 × savage_level; a march with no hero is limited to the squad deployment capacity. Set either to 0 to treat it as unlimited.
  3. Heroes take marches in the order shown in the Heroes section. Untick one to drop it from the split — its march falls back to the squad capacity, so the total sent goes down.
  4. Deployable total: T = min(T_troops, sum of every march cap), then distributed as evenly as possible. Capped marches are held at their cap and the remainder is redistributed across marches with room.
  5. Within each march: infantry = M × r_inf, cavalry = M × r_cav, archers = M × r_arc

Rounding uses largest-remainder plus a global reconciliation, so every march sums exactly to its total without using more troops than you own. Leave a capacity at 0 to treat it as unlimited.
