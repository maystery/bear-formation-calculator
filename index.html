Before you set these in game
The Bear Formation screen lets you set a ratio, but not exact troop counts, and its displayed capacity may exclude rally bonuses. Use this calculator to choose the ratio, then compare the final in-game troop counts with Check a march. A full rally or too few archers can still change the final mix.

How it's calculated
  1. Troop ceiling — the scarcest type relative to its share: T_troops = min(Inf / r_inf, Cav / r_cav, Arc / r_arc)
  2. Each march has its own cap: a march with a hero leader uses base deploy cap + 3,000 × savage_level; a march with no hero is limited to the squad deployment capacity. Set either to 0 to treat it as unlimited.
  3. Heroes take marches in this priority order: Amadeus, Chenko, Yeonwoo, Amane, then Hilde. Untick one to drop it from the split — its march falls back to the squad capacity, so the total sent goes down.
  4. Deployable total: T = min(T_troops, sum of every march cap). Hero-led rallies are filled first, evenly up to their individual caps. Any troops left after that are distributed evenly among no-hero rallies.
  5. Within each march: infantry = M × r_inf, cavalry = M × r_cav, archers = M × r_arc

Rounding uses largest-remainder plus a global reconciliation, so every march sums exactly to its total without using more troops than you own. Leave a capacity at 0 to treat it as unlimited.
