Formula
1. Max deployable total: T = min(Inf / r_inf, Cav / r_cav, Arc / r_arc) — the scarcest troop type relative to its share sets the ceiling.
2. With a max march capacity C over n marches: T = min(T, C × n) — and the number of marches you can fill completely is floor(T_troops / C).
3. Per march: M = T / n, then infantry = M × r_inf, cavalry = M × r_cav, archers = M × r_arc
Rounding uses largest-remainder so the per-march numbers sum exactly to the total.
