## Generation prompts

Each portrait used the following prompt with its filename stem for `{name}` and the corresponding costume below.

```text
Create a transparent, full-color holiday costume portrait for the fantasy game hero {name}, editing the provided source portrait. New Build is a festive winter workshop collection. Costume: {costume}. Keep this exact hero's recognizable identity, face, skin, expression, proportions, pose, camera angle, original full-body crop and original subject placement. Match the original polished stylized 3D game rendering. Make the costume and head details clearly recognizable even in a tiny avatar. Subtle warm forge light. Keep edges crisp and complete, with actual transparency everywhere outside the silhouette. Vertical 4:5 canvas, no background, lettering, frame or floating effects.
```

## Winter banner and capacity buffs

The built-in image generator created the [workshop panorama](../assets/holidays/winter/workshop.webp) and used the classic Valora and Mighty Bison portraits as individual references for the [buff artwork](../assets/holidays/winter/buffs/). Each WebP has its source PNG alongside it.

### Valora

```text
Edit the supplied Valora portrait for a winter festival in a fantasy mobile game. Preserve her warm brown skin, undercut and long brown braid, bright white stripe across her cheeks, amber eyes and exact facial features, gaze, head position and bust framing. Dress her as the winter hunt captain: deep evergreen velvet cloak, ivory shearling collar, cranberry red clasp ribbon, polished brass snowflake shoulder armor and a tiny sprig of holly tucked at the base of her braid. Match the original sculpted 3D game rendering, crisp shape language and studio highlights. Square, same close-up composition, with clean genuine transparent background and no detached decorations or lettering.
```

### Mighty Bison

```text
Edit this Mighty Bison square bust portrait into his winter festival costume. Preserve his recognizable friendly expression, blue-grey fur, enormous pink muzzle, bright blue eyes, dark fluffy forelock, natural curved tan horns, pose, head shape, proportions and square framing. Give him a thick cranberry red knitted scarf woven with simple ivory snowflakes, an evergreen sprig and two polished brass sleigh bells at its knot. A tiny jaunty evergreen knit cap between horns shows a cream pompom. Keep both horns clear and fully visible and accessories readable at thumbnail size. Match the polished, softly lit 3D cartoon game art of the source. Clean actual transparency everywhere outside the bust, no scene, no floating effects, no text.
```

### Workshop banner

```text
Wide panoramic banner illustration for a fantasy mobile game's winter festival, 3:1 landscape. A cozy snow-covered medieval toymaker's workshop at blue hour with golden glowing mullioned windows, evergreen garlands, a deep cranberry bow and hanging brass star lantern over a cheery wooden door. Place this main large, attractive, readable building toward the RIGHT third of the panorama. A softly curving lantern-lit snow path, fir trees and timber fence anchor the middle, receding into a dark evergreen night forest at the LEFT, intentionally low detail with dark teal shadows to accommodate white UI title text that will be added by the site. Stylized polished 3D storybook diorama, tactile snow and carved timber, rich fir greens, cream snow, warm honey window light and small cranberry accents. A cozy atmospheric establishing shot, visually strong at 170 pixels high. No people, no letters, signs, typography, frame, borders, labels or watermarks. Fill the rectangular canvas edge to edge.
```
